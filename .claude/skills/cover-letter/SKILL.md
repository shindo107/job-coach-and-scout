# Cover Letter Workflow

**Load and execute:** `workflows/cover-letter/workflow.md`

Read the entire workflow file and execute it step by step. This workflow:

1. Loads your Resume Corpus, job posting, and tailored resume
2. Analyzes your writing samples for voice matching
3. Generates a draft cover letter in your authentic voice
4. Conducts iterative refinement based on your feedback
5. Saves the final letter to applications/cover_letters/

Follow all steps exactly as written. Focus on capturing the user's authentic voice while highlighting what hiring managers need to see.
